Fifth is First – The Film

I had the honor to join the crew at Fifth Street Cross for the 2025 season and document its 20th anniversary: For twenty years, the Fifth Street Cross Series has embodied a simple truth: Sport is for everyone. What began as a backyard cyclocross race has grown into a community where cyclists—and now runners—of all backgrounds test their limits and fight for a championship that redefines what it means to win.
